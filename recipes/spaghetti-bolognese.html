Spaghetti Bolognese

A delicious Italian pasta dish -- (magnific.com)

Spaghetti Bolognese is a beloved Italian-inspired dish that combines long, thin spaghetti pasta with a rich, slow-cooked meat sauce known as ragù alla bolognese. Originating from the city of Bologna, the authentic sauce is traditionally made with a mixture of ground beef or pork, pancetta, vegetables (onion, carrot, celery), tomato paste or a touch of passata, white wine, and milk or cream, all simmered to develop deep, savory flavors

However, a common point of culinary debate is that true Bolognese is rarely served with spaghetti in Italy, as the thin pasta doesn't hold the heavy sauce well; instead, it's typically paired with wider, sturdier noodles like tagliatelle or pappardelle. Nevertheless, the version known worldwide as "Spaghetti Bolognese" has become a comforting, family-friendly staple in many countries, often topped with grated Parmesan or Pecorino cheese.

Ingredients

  • Meat (ground beef or pork)
  • Soffritto (onion, carrot, celery)
  • Tomato paste or passata
  • Dry white wine and a splash of cream or milk
  • Broth: Meat broth of beef stock
  • Seasoning: Salt, pepper, and optionally nutmeg
  • Olive oil or Butter
  • Pasta (spaghetti)

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil or butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the soffritto (onion, carrot, celery) and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the ground beef or pork and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks, about 8-10 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes to deepen the flavors.
  4. Pour in the dry white wine and let it simmer for 2-3 minutes until slightly reduced.
  5. Add the meat broth and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat to low and let the sauce simmer for at least 30-45 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg to taste. Stir in the splash of cream or milk and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  7. While the sauce simmers, cook the spaghetti in a large pot of salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente. Drain well.
  8. Serve the spaghetti topped with the Bolognese sauce and grated Parmesan or Pecorino cheese.
